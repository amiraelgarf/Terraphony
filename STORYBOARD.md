**Narration lines** (on-screen, no voice-over): “We know how Earth looks. What if we could follow
how it changes — by listening?” · “You chose a line across the sea. Every point along it adds another
note.” · “Now stay in one place. Let the months move around you.” · “That change you heard? Here is
the observation that made it.” · “This is your path, pressed into a record.” · “A piece of Earth,
interpreted through your choices.”

## The narrated journey (one click)

All of these beats run without another click.

| Beat | Scene | Host line | What moves it on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 Invitation | arrival | “Welcome. Today we have a new kind of orchestra… the Earth itself.” | the cue ends → needle / ring / iris |
| 01 Choosing | library, Side A highlighted | “We could begin anywhere. For our first performance, let's follow a line through the ocean.” | the visitor picks a side (their choice wins), or the cue ends → the card zooms into the studio |
| 02 First performance | ocean studio, Gulf Stream, 18 Aug 2007 | “Follow the path. Each stop becomes a note… Listen.” | the cue ends → music plays |
| 03 Discovery | the music stops one note after stop 9 | “There. Did you hear the turn?…” + the exact values | the cues end → “Let's hear the rest.” → the music resumes |
| 04 Baton | end of the performance | “My turn is over. Now the baton is yours…” | → one next action: draw a route / another place → Press |
| 05 Record ready | reveal | “A record unlike any other. Earth provided the pattern. You chose where to listen.” | the visitor pressed |

## 90-second judge demo

1. 00:00 Invitation → **Make my Earth Record** (needle, ring → line).
2. 00:10 **Ocean in Motion** → **Hear this journey**: cursor, score and melody move together.
3. 00:25 Pause on the biggest drop — stop 9, 39.9° N 59.5° W, 28.3 → 23.7 °C on 18 Aug 2007
   (the tour's “Show me” finds it automatically) → inspector: value, °C, day, place, note →
   *Technical details*: “The sound is artistic. The number behind it isn't invented.”
4. 00:35 **Draw a route** across the Indian Ocean; rename it; choose *Felt & dusk*.
5. 00:50 **Press my Earth Record** → line → score → groove → sleeve → **Play my record**, show the PNG/JSON.
6. 01:03 **A Year in One Place** → Nile Delta dial: two harvests; switch to Harvard Forest: the snow rest.
7. 01:20 **How it works**: sources, ranges, credits. “Every record has a story, and every note has an origin.”
